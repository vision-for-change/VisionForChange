Our Mission

Access to eye care shouldn't depend on your ability to pay.

Vision for Change is built around one belief: essential eye care is a basic need, not a luxury. We work on prevention, access, and education at the same time, because barriers rarely arrive one at a time.

We are a youth-led, volunteer-run initiative. We are not a clinic and we are not a medical provider. What we do is connect people to care, build tools that make prevention easier, and make eye-health information clearer.

Get Eye-Care Assistance

Need help accessing eye care?

If cost or another barrier is preventing you from accessing eye care, Vision for Change may be able to connect you with available resources and participating providers.

Requesting assistance is free. We never ask for payment information, and we only share your details with a provider if you want us to.

01How it works

Three steps, no cost to you.

1

Tell us about your situation

Complete our short assistance form.

2

We review your request

We'll determine what resources or partnerships may be appropriate.

3

We connect you

If we can assist, we'll work to connect you with an appropriate resource or participating provider.

Request Assistance
We're an early-stage initiative still building our provider network, so we can't guarantee an outcome for every request. We'll do our best to connect you with available assistance.
02Common questions

Before you reach out

Who can apply?

Anyone facing a financial or practical barrier to accessing eye care. You don't need to prove anything up front. Just tell us honestly about your situation.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Requesting assistance is free, and we never ask for payment information.

How long does it take?

We review requests as they come in. Because we're volunteer-run, response times can vary, and we'll keep you updated.

Is my information private?

We only share what's necessary to connect you with a resource or participating provider, and only with your knowledge.

About Us

Built by young people who believe vision matters.

Vision for Change began with a simple idea: access to quality eye care should not depend solely on someone's financial circumstances.

Founded in 2026 in Ottawa, Canada. Youth-led, volunteer-run, and working on prevention, access, and education at the same time.

01Why we started

A pair of glasses changes everything.

For a lot of people, an eye exam and a pair of glasses is the difference between struggling through a classroom or a workday and simply being able to see. It's one of the most solvable problems in health care, and it still isn't solved, mostly because of cost and access.

We started Vision for Change because that gap felt both unfair and fixable. We're young, we're volunteer-run, and we don't have the resources of a large charity. What we do have is the ability to build tools, talk to people, and connect the ones who need care with the ones who can provide it.

"Access to quality eye care should not depend solely on someone's financial circumstances."

That's the whole thesis. Everything we build, the app, the partnerships, the education work, comes back to it.

Privacy, terms and accessibility

Plain-language summaries of how we handle your information and what you can expect from this site.

Questions about any of this? Email us at visionforchange18@gmail.com and we'll answer directly.

Privacy

We collect only what you send us directly: your name, contact details, and what you tell us about your situation. We use it to respond to you and, with your knowledge, to connect you with a resource or participating provider. We do not sell your information.

Terms

Vision for Change provides information and referrals. We are not a medical provider, and nothing on this site or in our app is medical advice, a diagnosis, or a substitute for an examination by a qualified eye-care professional.

Accessibility

We aim to keep this site usable with a keyboard and a screen reader, with readable contrast and no motion that cannot be turned off. If something isn't working for you, email us and we'll fix it.
